Site Purpose

To provide information about Burgers'n Scoops, including menu items, costs, location, contact details, and employment information.

To create an online presence, to support those people who want to look up information, rather than call to ask questions, such as menu items, costs, time of operation, and placing an order.

Scenarios

The scenarios are questions that the target audience might ask when visiting the website.

  • Scenario 1: What kinds of food do they have, such as flavors of shakes or different kinds of fries?
  • Scenario 2: I have seen the menu and want to order without waiting in a line. How do I place an order online?
  • Scenario 3: I have friends who work for Burgers'n Scoops, how do I apply for a job? Include the process, contact information, and application. Would it be helpful to include a meeting scheduler to schedule interviews?
  • Scenario 4: I really like the food, but I'm on a budget. What is the cost of the menu items? With this question it would be important to consider how to keep the costs updated. Maybe use a json file with information of the menu items' costs, so that the costs can be updated in real time.
