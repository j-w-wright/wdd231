Week 4
Subject: Fast Food Restaurant
Description: A website for the restaurant that includes pages for Home, Menu, Deals, Careers, and Gift Cards.  Also, provide the inclusion of an option to order online and have orders delivered online.
Reason & Purpose: There is a fast food restaurant in town that my daughter just began working for and the owner is in my ward.  I noticed that the restaurant doesn't have a website and thought this would give me a good opportunity to work on creating a site for a business that is already in business.  I could also get feedback from my daughter who will have particular insights regarding the restaurant.
